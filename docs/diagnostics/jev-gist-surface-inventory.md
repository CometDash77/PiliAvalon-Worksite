### Shared filtering details

- RecommendFilter.filter combines configured duration, play-count, like-ratio, derived metrics, and legacy title regex gates: [implementation](https://github.com/CometDash77/PiliAvalon-Worksite/blob/3f7d5c83f6c6489d1f87340974dc45bd10743308/lib/utils/recommend_filter.dart#L36-L95).
- Recommendation shielding uses ShieldingAdapters and ShieldMatcher; related cards use a distinct scope/switch: [adapter](https://github.com/CometDash77/PiliAvalon-Worksite/blob/3f7d5c83f6c6489d1f87340974dc45bd10743308/lib/features/shielding/shielding_adapters.dart#L169-L200).
- Detail-tag filtering performs per-video UserHttp.videoTags requests with bounded concurrency and fail-open behavior: [enricher](https://github.com/CometDash77/PiliAvalon-Worksite/blob/3f7d5c83f6c6489d1f87340974dc45bd10743308/lib/features/shielding/shielding_recommend_tag_enricher.dart#L73-L220).
- The Home-only comment gate is optional, network-backed, bounded, and fail-open: [gate](https://github.com/CometDash77/PiliAvalon-Worksite/blob/3f7d5c83f6c6489d1f87340974dc45bd10743308/lib/features/shielding/home_feed_comment_gate.dart#L18-L103).
- The Home-only exposure tracker filters and records candidate exposures: [tracker](https://github.com/CometDash77/PiliAvalon-Worksite/blob/3f7d5c83f6c6489d1f87340974dc45bd10743308/lib/features/exposure_tracker/exposure_tracker.dart#L43-L82).

- PGC/Bangumi and Cinema tabs render a section explicitly titled “推荐”, backed by PgcHttp.pgcIndex and PgcIndexItem, not the standard video candidate path: [controller](https://github.com/CometDash77/PiliAvalon-Worksite/blob/3f7d5c83f6c6489d1f87340974dc45bd10743308/lib/pages/pgc/controller.dart#L16-L25) and [section](https://github.com/CometDash77/PiliAvalon-Worksite/blob/3f7d5c83f6c6489d1f87340974dc45bd10743308/lib/pages/pgc/view.dart#L208-L320).
- Music detail has a BGM recommendation list from MusicHttp.bgmRecommend, using BgmRecommend: [controller](https://github.com/CometDash77/PiliAvalon-Worksite/blob/3f7d5c83f6c6489d1f87340974dc45bd10743308/lib/pages/music/video/controller.dart#L10-L32) and [HTTP](https://github.com/CometDash77/PiliAvalon-Worksite/blob/3f7d5c83f6c6489d1f87340974dc45bd10743308/lib/http/music.dart#L46-L60).

The Notes do not explicitly settle whether these PGC and music routes count as Jev surfaces. A new user-in-loop ticket, [Decide Whether PGC and Music Recommendation Surfaces Belong in Jev Scope](https://github.com/CometDash77/PiliAvalon-Worksite/issues/43), records that open decision and blocks the settings prototype and quality/cost evaluation until settled. Other pages with “recommendation” in names return topics or specialized entities rather than video-feed candidates and were not promoted as Jev video surfaces based on this inventory.

## Resolution

For the currently confirmed surfaces, Jev can only be added after each path’s existing eligibility and shielding work. Home Web/App additionally have tag enrichment, optional comment screening, and exposure filtering; related, hot, and each ranking family have separate pipelines. The live path is independent and should be coordinated through map #19. PGC and music inclusion remains an explicit user decision in ticket #43.
